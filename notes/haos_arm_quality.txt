HAOS arm quality
Campaign: data/results/lagrange_benchmark
Runs: 300. Instances: 100.

Definition
  One row is one draw whose BG-AILS candidate came back.
  C_t for iteration t >= 1 is dr_apply.incumbent_cost, the live best after
  the previous draw's BG-AILS result has been drained and before this draw's
  decompose-route candidate. At iteration 0 the opening decompose-route cost
  is C_0. That opening candidate is not a row. Iteration 0's BG-AILS call is.
  Quality = 100 * (C_t - bg_cost) / C_t, signed, in percentage points.
  A new incumbent is the logged adoption of this draw's decompose-route
  candidate (iterations >= 1 only) or of its BG-AILS candidate.
  Repair gain = (dr_cost - bg_cost) / dr_cost. It is not an arm score.
  Cap-skipped launches with no BG-AILS candidate: 630. They are out.
  Rows: 22173. Warm-up rows (iterations 0-9): 3000.
  The k test uses arms 2, 3, 4, 6, 8, 10. Dropped: 12, which is missing on
  X-n1654-k11, and every extension arm.
  Extension and other dropped k values present in the rows: [11, 12, 14, 15, 16, 17, 18, 19, 20, 21, 23, 24, 25, 26, 27, 28, 32, 36, 40].
  Position control: within each instance, outcome ~ arm + centered iteration.
  The reported arm value is the fitted mean at that instance's mean iteration,
  then averaged across the 100 instances. Draws are weighted equally.
  The three seeds of an instance are pooled before the fit.
  Friedman treats instances as blocks and arms as treatments.
  The paradigm wheel has two arms, so that row is a Wilcoxon signed-rank
  test of the paired instance difference instead of Friedman.
  Bootstrap: 2000 resamples of instances, seed 20260923. The best and
  worst arms are re-selected inside each resample. Intervals are percentiles.
  The noise floor is the Section 4.1.4 per-instance seed standard deviation, 0.037 pp.
