  probability of the arm credited on this wheel, with a 0 when the wheel was not drawn.
  For the method wheels that 0 is the iterations of the other paradigm.

Diagnostic 5. Deferred credit as a flattening force
  An iteration is in the deferred group when an SC/SP apply in that iteration has a
  positive recomputed deferred reward (7 for a new best, 3 for an improvement on the
  last cost). A deferred reward of 0 does not change a weight and stays with the other
  iterations. Per-arm targets are not logged. What is compared is already in the weights:
  how many arms are strictly heavier than in the previous snapshot, and the credit mass
  implied by w_t = gamma (w_(t-1) + credits_t) where the floor does not bind.
  Injected mass is the identified credit: exact on arms that finished above the floor,
  and the known immediate credit on arms that finished on it. The floor slack is the

Deferred share of credit mass
  Above the floor, credits_t = w_t / gamma - w_(t-1), summed over arms. That part is a point.
  On a floored arm the identity only bounds the credit by w0/gamma - w_prev.
