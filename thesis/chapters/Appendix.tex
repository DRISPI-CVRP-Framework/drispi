\section{Set Covering Formulation}\label{app:set-covering}
Section~\ref{sec:fra-sp} introduces set covering (SC) as the fallback DRISPI runs in place of set partitioning (SP) when the route pool does not yet cover every customer at least a minimum number of times. With $R$ the route pool at the time of the solve, $c_r$ the cost of route $r \in R$, $C$ the customer set of the instance (Chapter~\ref{chapter:problem}), $C_r \subseteq C$ the customer set of route $r$, and $x_r \in \{0, 1\}$ selecting route $r$, SC relaxes the partition equality of SP (Equation~\ref{eq:sp-partition}) to the cover inequality of \citet{garfinkelSetPartitioningProblemSetCovering1969}, who characterize set partitioning as set covering under an equality constraint:
\begin{align}
\min \quad & \sum_{r \in R} c_r x_r \label{eq:sc-obj}\\
\text{s.t.} \quad & \sum_{r \in R:\, i \in C_r} x_r \geq 1, && \forall i \in C \label{eq:sc-cover}\\
& x_r \in \{0, 1\}, && \forall r \in R. \label{eq:sc-binary}
\end{align}
As with SP, DRISPI solves the LP relaxation of Equation~\ref{eq:sc-binary}, $x_r \in [0, 1]$, first; the resulting fractional weights update the pool's quality scores in the same way regardless of whether the solve ends up running SC or SP, and the binary re-solve is warm-started from that relaxation.
